# Tests

## Test 1 - Simplified homepage routes donors to one clear action [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the locked hero remains intact and the How it works section gains a readable senior-smartphone photo without adding homepage complexity.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Confirm the donation-process photo, blue hero treatment, white "Old Phones. New Possibilities." headline, and primary "Donate a Phone" action are visible
3. Scroll to How it works and confirm the senior-couple smartphone photo appears; continue down and confirm the black Labrador mascot appears while phone-condition and donor address fields remain absent
Expected: The homepage keeps the locked blue-and-white hero, adds a relevant photo beside How it works, keeps the mascot as a secondary guide lower on the page, and routes visitors to the dedicated donation flow without displaying the full transaction workflow.

## Test 2 - Pledge charity search appears in the donation form
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the configured public partner key loads the official single-organization Pledge search in Step 3.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /donate-phone.html
2. Advance through the phone and estimate stages to Step 3
3. Find the heading "Choose the charity that your donation supports."
4. Confirm the Pledge nonprofit search area is visible
Expected: The charity-search section loads without exposing a secret API key and allows one nonprofit selection.

## Test 3 - Charity selection is required before submission
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the donation packet cannot be submitted without a Pledge organization UUID.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /donate-phone.html and advance to Step 3
2. Fill all required donor fields and check the phone-preparation confirmation
3. Do not select a charity
4. Tap "Create donation packet"
Expected: Focus moves to the charity section and an inline message says "Please select the charity you would like your donation to support."

## Test 4 - Selected charity remains visible and can be changed
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies mocked Pledge selection and removal events update the visible form state.

3. Confirm the selected-charity panel shows the nonprofit name and Pledge ID
4. Tap "Change charity" or dispatch removeEvent
