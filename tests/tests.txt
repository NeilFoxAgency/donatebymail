# Tests

## Test 1 - Estimate a phone and see a value range [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the main estimator loads and produces a visible estimate.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Scroll to "Estimate your phone"
3. Change condition to "Good"
4. Confirm the estimate card updates
Expected: A dollar range is visible beside "Estimated device value" and no error appears.

## Test 2 - Add a second phone
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies donors can include more than one device on a small screen.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Scroll to "Estimate your phone"
3. Tap "Add another phone"
4. Confirm two phone cards are shown
Expected: Two numbered phone cards are visible and the total estimate reflects both devices.

## Test 3 - Shipping request guardrail
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the preparation confirmation is required before the preview request completes.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Scroll to "Request your prepaid mailing plan"
3. Fill in name, email, and ZIP code
4. Tap "Create my mailing plan" without checking the preparation confirmation
Expected: The success panel does not appear and the required confirmation remains incomplete.
