# Tests

## Test 1 - Simplified homepage routes donors to one clear action [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the homepage is shorter and keeps the full estimator and donor form off the homepage.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Confirm the hero, three-step overview, and primary "Donate a phone" action are visible
3. Confirm the homepage does not show phone-condition fields or donor address fields
Expected: The homepage explains the mission and routes the visitor to a dedicated donation page without displaying the full transaction workflow.

## Test 2 - Donation flow reveals one stage at a time
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the dedicated Donate a Phone page uses a clear four-stage sequence.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /donate-phone.html
2. Confirm Step 1 shows only phone-detail fields
3. Tap "See my estimate"
4. Confirm Step 2 shows the combined estimate and no donor-address form
Expected: The progress indicator advances from Phone to Estimate and unrelated later-stage fields remain hidden.

## Test 3 - Donor can complete the staged packet workflow
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the main donation workflow reaches printable documents.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /donate-phone.html
2. Advance from the phone step to the estimate step and then to mailing details
3. Fill all required donor fields and check the preparation confirmation
4. Tap "Create donation packet"
Expected: Step 4 displays a donation ID, Packing slip tab, Acknowledgment preview tab, and print actions.

## Test 4 - Preparation confirmation blocks completion
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the required phone-preparation acknowledgment remains a guardrail.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /donate-phone.html and advance to Step 3
2. Fill all required donor fields
3. Leave the preparation confirmation unchecked and tap "Create donation packet"
Expected: The browser keeps the donor on Step 3 and does not display the document step.

## Test 5 - Older-audience mobile controls remain readable
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the simplified layout, progress indicator, and form controls fit a small screen.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /donate-phone.html
2. Review the progress indicator and phone-detail controls
3. Add another phone and scroll through both records
Expected: Controls are full width, labels remain visible, buttons do not overflow, and there is no horizontal scrolling.

## Test 6 - Header and footer remain consistent across page systems
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies React pages and static information pages use the same nonprofit notice, navigation, mission statement, contact email, and footer groups.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the homepage and note the header navigation and footer headings
2. Navigate to /donate-phone.html and confirm the same header and footer content
3. Navigate to /prepare-phone.html and confirm the same header and footer content
4. Navigate to /about.html and confirm the same header and footer content
Expected: All four pages show the identical charity notice, six primary navigation links, mission statement, EIN, contact email, and Get started, Learn, and Organization and policies footer groups.
