# Tests

## Test 1 - Mission, nonprofit identity, and hero photography are clear [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the redesigned homepage immediately communicates the mission and uses documentary photography in the hero.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Wait for the homepage to load
3. Confirm the main heading, right-side phone-packing photograph, and nonprofit statement are visible
Expected: The page says an old phone can still do useful work, identifies Donate by Mail as a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity, and shows a clear phone-preparation photograph on the right side of the hero.

## Test 2 - Crawlable resource page is useful on mobile
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the redesigned static resource page provides substantial original content.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /resources.html
2. Confirm the preparation guidance is visible
3. Expand one frequently asked question
Expected: The resource page displays phone-preparation guidance and a readable FAQ answer without horizontal scrolling.

## Test 3 - Multi-phone estimator updates
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the workbench supports multiple phones and a combined estimate.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Scroll to the phone estimator
3. Tap "Add another phone"
4. Confirm two phone records are visible
Expected: Two numbered phone records and a combined informational estimate are visible.

## Test 4 - Required preparation confirmation blocks completion
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the donor packet cannot be created without the data-preparation confirmation.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Scroll to the donor record form
3. Fill all required donor fields
4. Submit without checking the preparation confirmation
Expected: The browser keeps the user on the donation form and does not show a completed donor packet.

## Test 5 - Photography remains responsive on mobile
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies the hero and mission photography stack cleanly without causing horizontal overflow.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Review the hero photograph and scroll to the final mission photograph
3. Confirm the text, image crops, and call-to-action button remain readable
Expected: The photographs fit within the mobile viewport, the closing photo stacks above the red message panel, and the page has no horizontal scrolling.

## Test 6 - Contact information is transparent
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the organization provides a direct contact path and explanatory content.
Steps:
1. Navigate to /contact.html
2. Review the email and inquiry guidance
3. Confirm the site-wide policy links are visible
Expected: A direct organization email, nonprofit inquiry guidance, and Privacy, Terms, and Accessibility links are visible.
