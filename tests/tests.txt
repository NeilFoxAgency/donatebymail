# Tests

## Test 1 - Estimate a phone and create a donation packet [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the main donor workflow creates a visible donation ID and document center.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Confirm a dollar range appears in the phone estimator
3. Fill all required donor and address fields
4. Check the data-preparation confirmation and submit
5. Scroll to the document center
Expected: A unique donation ID, packing slip tab, and acknowledgment preview tab are visible.

## Test 2 - Add a second phone and update the total
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies donors can include multiple devices and see a combined estimate on a small screen.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Scroll to the phone estimator
3. Tap "Add another phone"
4. Change the second phone brand to Samsung
Expected: Two numbered phone cards are visible and the combined estimate reflects both devices.

## Test 3 - Required preparation confirmation blocks submission
Viewport: mobile
Description: Verifies a donor cannot create the packet without confirming data-preparation responsibilities.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Fill all required donor and address fields
3. Leave the preparation confirmation unchecked
4. Tap "Create my donation packet"
Expected: The document center does not appear and the required checkbox remains incomplete.
