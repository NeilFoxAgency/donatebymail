# Donate by Mail design direction

This design pass studies the principles in Nutlope's Hallmark repository and applies them to Donate by Mail without copying any Hallmark example, theme, or page.

## Structural direction

The site uses a Long Document + Workbench structure:

- a masthead and utility line rather than the standard sticky SaaS navigation
- a reading-led hero paired with an operational donation docket
- a ruled, numbered process instead of equal feature cards
- a dark workbench for the estimator and device records
- a long-form security section rather than icon tiles
- an institutional donor form with fieldsets and hairline rules
- a letter-close call to action and colophon-style footer

## Anti-patterns intentionally removed

- gradient and blurred-orb hero decoration
- hero badge or pill
- equal four-card feature grid
- icon tiles above every heading
- nested rounded cards
- pill-shaped buttons throughout
- sticky white navigation with a CTA on the far right
- eyebrow labels on every section
- repeated centered section composition
- large soft shadows and floating surfaces

## Brand and typography

The red, white, and blue brand remains, but the colors behave like institutional inks rather than app-interface effects.

- Navy is the primary ink and workbench surface.
- Red is reserved for action, status, and postal emphasis.
- Warm paper neutrals replace pure white and blue-grey SaaS backgrounds.
- Display type uses an old-style serif stack.
- Body and forms use an Avenir-led sans-serif stack.
- Headings remain roman, never italic.

## Interaction rules

- Controls use small radii and clear borders.
- Hover is never the only affordance.
- Focus states remain visible.
- Motion is limited to brief color changes and scrolling requested by the donor.
- Printable documents retain a separate, formal visual voice.
- The layout is verified conceptually for 320, 375, 414, and 768 pixel widths.

## Future additions

Photography should be added only when Donate by Mail has authentic images of donated devices, mail kits, nonprofit partners, or staff. Stock-style AI imagery would weaken the trust this direction is designed to create.
