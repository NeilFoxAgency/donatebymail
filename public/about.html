About Donate by Mail

Donate by Mail is a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity founded by Neil Fox to make phone donation easier for individuals and more practical for charities.

Start a phone donation

Our mission

Turn unused phones into funding for meaningful causes. The program connects donors, reusable technology, and nonprofit fundraising through a guided mail-in process.

Why this work matters

Many phones remain unused after an upgrade even though some retain reuse, parts, or resale value. Charities also need accessible fundraising programs that do not require them to become device-processing organizations.
