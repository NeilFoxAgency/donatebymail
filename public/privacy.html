How information is handled on this website

Privacy policy

Effective July 20, 2026. This policy covers the public site and current browser-based donor-packet workflow.

Start a phone donation

Information you provide

The donor tool may request a name, email, mailing address, and phone descriptions. In the current preview, this remains in the browser unless you choose to send a prepared email.

Browser-saved information

Save-draft and donor-packet features use local storage. You may remove this data by clearing site data in your browser.

Email and analytics

Email actions open your email application for review. When an authorized GA4 ID is configured, visitors are offered a choice before analytics initializes. The site works if analytics is declined.

Sharing and security

Donate by Mail does not sell donor personal information. Production providers may receive information only as reasonably needed for shipping, processing, communications, accounting, legal compliance, and security.

Questions and choices

Decline analytics, clear browser data, choose not to email a request, or ask a privacy question at satoshi@donatebymail.org.
