Privacy policy

This policy covers the public site and current browser-based donor-packet workflow.

Information you provide

The donor tool requests your first and last name, optional middle name, email address, country and mailing address, phone descriptions, charity selection, and email preference. When you create a donation packet, this information is sent to Donate by Mail and the service providers needed to process the packet.

Browser-saved information

Save-draft and donor-packet features use local storage. You may remove this information by clearing site data.

Email and analytics

We send essential emails about donation confirmation and status as part of processing your donation. A separate, unchecked box lets you opt in to occasional updates. You may decline without affecting your donation and may withdraw that choice through an unsubscribe link or by emailing tre@donatebymail.org. The site remains functional without optional analytics.

Sharing and security

Donate by Mail does not sell donor personal information. Production providers may receive information only as reasonably needed for shipping, processing, accounting, legal compliance, and security.

Questions

Email tre@donatebymail.org with privacy questions.
