How phone donation works

The program is designed to make noncash phone donations understandable for donors and manageable for nonprofit partners.

Start a phone donation

1. Describe the phones

Record the manufacturer, model when known, age, storage, condition, power status, and activation-lock status. This supports an informational estimate and a useful intake description.

2. Prepare personal data

Back up needed files, sign out of accounts, disable device-finding features, remove SIM and memory cards, and factory reset when possible. Never mail passwords or passcodes.

3. Request mailing materials

Create a packing slip and request a prepaid label or mailed kit. The packing slip identifies the donation inside the package.

4. Record physical receipt

An acknowledgment is not final until Donate by Mail physically receives and verifies the property. Staff records the actual receipt date and compares the package with the donor description.

5. Reuse, resell, or responsibly recycle

The pathway depends on safety, device condition, account locks, parts value, and market demand.

6. Report results

For participating charity campaigns, 50% of net proceeds is directed to the charity partner after applicable shipping, processing, resale, marketplace, and program costs.
