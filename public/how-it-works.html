A clear path from drawer to donation

Here is what happens to your phone.

Donate by Mail keeps the process in a simple order: tell us what you have, choose the nonprofit you want to support, prepare the phone, mail it, and let us handle the rest.

The full journey

Six steps, with no mystery in the middle.

You know what you are doing now and what happens next.

01

Tell us about the phone

Choose the manufacturer, model when known, age, storage, condition, whether it powers on, and whether it is carrier-unlocked. You can add several phones to one donation.

The estimate shown in the flow is for planning only. It is not a tax appraisal.
02

Choose the charity you want to support

Search the nonprofit directory in the donation flow and select one organization. The charity stays attached to the donation packet so it can be identified when the phones arrive.

Double-check the organization name, location, or EIN when available before continuing.
03

Prepare your data and accounts

Back up anything you want to keep, sign out of Apple or Google accounts, turn off activation locks, remove physical SIM and memory cards, and factory reset the phone when possible.

Use our preparation guide before sealing the box.
04

Buy postage and pack the phone

The donation flow creates your donation ID, packing slip, and a mailing label addressed to Donate By Mail. Use your own sturdy box, choose and pay a carrier, power the phone off, protect it from movement, and keep the donation ID with the package.

Do not mail a visibly swollen, leaking, punctured, or overheating battery. Contact us first.
05

We receive and verify what arrived

Donate by Mail records the physical receipt, confirms the device count, and compares the package with the donor description. Only then can a charitable acknowledgment be finalized.

The acknowledgment does not assign a fair market value to your donated property.
06

The phone follows the most useful responsible path

Depending on condition, account locks, parts value, safety, and market demand, a device may be reused, resold, used for parts, or responsibly recycled. For participating campaigns, the applicable proceeds share and eligible costs are defined in the campaign's approved terms.

See how we describe proceeds and reporting.

Before the box is sealed

Doggo says: protect your data first.

If there is only one thing you slow down for, make it phone preparation. Back up your files and confirm you can access your important accounts somewhere else before you erase anything.

After arrival

Reuse when possible. Recycle responsibly when it is not.

Not every old phone has the same next chapter. A working device with resale demand may generate more charitable funding. A damaged device may be useful for parts. A phone that cannot be reused should be kept out of the ordinary waste stream and directed toward responsible electronics recycling.

The important part is that the outcome is based on the phone that actually arrives, not an optimistic estimate made before inspection.
