These terms explain the informational website and donor workflow.

Informational estimates

Value ranges are not offers, guaranteed proceeds, appraisals, or tax valuations.

Donor authority

The donor represents that they own the phone or are authorized to donate it and that it is not stolen, leased, or unlawfully transferred.

Data responsibility

Donors are responsible for backups, account sign-outs, lock removal, SIM removal, and factory resets when possible.

Charitable transfer

Phones are charitable contributions, not trade-ins. Donors do not receive cash or store credit.

Acknowledgments and taxes

An official acknowledgment is issued after physical receipt and verification. Donate by Mail describes property but does not determine fair market value.
