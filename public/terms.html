Important terms for estimates, devices, and acknowledgments

Website and donation terms

Effective July 20, 2026. These terms explain the current informational website and donor workflow.

Start a phone donation

Informational estimates

Value ranges are not offers, guaranteed proceeds, appraisals, or tax valuations. Actual results may be higher, lower, or zero.

Donor authority

The donor represents that they own the phone or are authorized to donate it and that it is not stolen, leased, or unlawfully transferred.

Data responsibility

Donors are responsible for backups, account sign-outs, activation-lock removal, SIM and memory-card removal, and factory resets when possible.

Charitable transfer

Phones are charitable contributions, not consumer trade-ins. Donors do not receive cash or store credit. Accepted property may be reused, resold, used for parts, or recycled.

Acknowledgments and taxes

An official acknowledgment is issued after physical receipt and verification. Donate by Mail describes property but does not determine fair market value.
