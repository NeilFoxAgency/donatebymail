Co-founders

Two different strengths, one practical mission.

Donate by Mail sits at the intersection of technology, shipping, operations, donor support, and nonprofit fundraising. The founding team reflects that mix.

Co-founder & President

Neil Fox

Neil focuses on the systems and partnerships behind Donate by Mail: the donor experience, website and workflows, nonprofit relationships, campaign development, reporting, and keeping the program understandable from beginning to end.

His professional background includes building and operating end-to-end e-commerce systems and business-analysis workflows, with hands-on experience connecting customer experience, fulfillment, analytics, and operations.

Co-founder & Director

Tre Plaisted

Tre brings a hands-on operations perspective to Donate by Mail. His background includes facilities support, transportation environments, maintenance, documentation, and practical problem-solving.

He helps keep the physical side of the program grounded in reality, including mailing and shipping considerations, operational procedures, device-handling needs, and the everyday details that determine whether a process is actually easy to use.

How we work

The program has to make sense on both sides of the mailbox.

Build the system

Design clear donor workflows, nonprofit partnerships, reporting, and technology around one understandable process.

Make it operational

Pressure-test the practical details of packing, mailing, handling, documentation, and day-to-day execution.

Respect the donor

Use plain language, protect personal data, and avoid asking people to understand electronics-industry jargon.

Report what happened

Base acknowledgments and program results on what was actually received and processed, not on optimistic forecasts.
