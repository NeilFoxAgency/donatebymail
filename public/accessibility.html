A site intended to work for as many people as possible

Accessibility statement

Donate by Mail aims to support keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, voice-control, touch, and other assistive-technology use.

Accessibility target

The site is designed toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA with semantic headings, labeled controls, visible focus, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and sufficient contrast.

Documents and printing

Packing slips and shipping labels use structured text and high-contrast print styles.

Known limitations

Email handoff requires an email application. Print and browser-storage behavior varies by browser.

Request help

Email tre@donatebymail.org with the page, task, device or assistive technology, and preferred response method.
