Clear information and careful reporting

Transparency

Trust depends on explaining the organization, financial model, receipt process, and limits of current data.

Tax-exempt status

Donate by Mail is recognized as a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity. EIN 92-1515120.

Proceeds model

For eligible phones in a participating campaign, the applicable proceeds share and eligible costs are defined in the campaign's approved terms.

What net proceeds means

Net proceeds are not the website estimate or gross resale amount. Actual results depend on condition, locks, demand, shipping, processing, fees, and recycling costs.

Receipt practices

Official acknowledgments are issued after physical receipt, describe the property, state whether goods or services were provided, and do not assign fair market value.

Impact claims

Devices, pathways, proceeds, costs, and distributions should be reported after the relevant activity is complete. Estimates are not presented as completed impact.
