Tax-exempt status

Donate by Mail is recognized as a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity. EIN 92-1515120.

Proceeds model

For eligible phones in a participating charity campaign, 50% of net proceeds is directed to the charity partner. Remaining net proceeds support Donate by Mail’s charitable program and operations.

What net proceeds means

Net proceeds are not the website estimate or gross resale amount. Actual results depend on condition, locks, demand, shipping, processing, marketplace fees, payment fees, and recycling costs.

Receipt practices

Official acknowledgments are issued after physical receipt, describe the property, state whether goods or services were provided, and do not assign the donor’s fair market value.

Impact claims

Phones received, device pathways, gross proceeds, costs, net proceeds, and partner distributions should be reported after the relevant activity is complete. Estimates are not presented as completed impact.
