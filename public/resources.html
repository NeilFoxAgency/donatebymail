Practical preparation guides and direct answers

Phone donation resources

Use these original guides to identify, prepare, reset, package, and document a phone donation.

Start a phone donation

Identify the phone

Open Settings and find About, About phone, or Device information. Record manufacturer, model, and storage. Keep IMEI and serial numbers private.

Remove account locks

Sign out of Apple ID, Google, and manufacturer accounts. Disable Find My or corresponding device protection.

Reset without losing needed files

Back up first, confirm authenticator access elsewhere, and then use the built-in erase or factory-reset process.

Package a lithium-ion device

Power it off, prevent movement, and use a sturdy box. Do not mail a visibly damaged or swollen battery.

Frequently asked questions

Is the estimate a tax deduction?

No. It is an informational resale estimate, not an appraisal or tax valuation.

When is the acknowledgment final?

After the organization physically receives and verifies the phones.

Can I include several phones?

Yes. The interactive tool supports multiple devices in one packet.

Do donors receive payment?

No. This is a charitable donation program, not a trade-in marketplace.
