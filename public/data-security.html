Protect your information

Phone data security

Donors remain responsible for backing up and removing personal information before a phone leaves their hands.

Back up what matters

Confirm photos, contacts, documents, and authenticator access are available elsewhere.

Sign out and remove locks

Sign out of Apple ID, Google, Samsung, and manufacturer accounts. Disable Find My or equivalent protections.

Factory reset

Use the built-in erase process and never mail a PIN, password, or backup code.

Inspect and package safely

Remove SIM and memory cards. Do not mail a swollen, leaking, punctured, smoking, or unusually hot battery.
