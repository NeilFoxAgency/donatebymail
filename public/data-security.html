Protect your information before a phone leaves your hands

Phone data security

Donors remain responsible for backing up and removing personal information. These steps reduce risk and improve the chance that a device can be reused.

Start a phone donation

Back up what matters

Confirm that photos, contacts, documents, and authenticator access are available on another device or trusted backup.

Sign out and remove locks

Sign out of Apple ID, Google, Samsung, and manufacturer accounts. Disable Find My or the corresponding device-protection feature.

Factory reset

Use the built-in erase or reset process and let the phone return to the initial setup screen. Never send a PIN, password, or backup code.

Remove cards and inspect the battery

Take out SIM and memory cards. Do not mail a swollen, leaking, punctured, smoking, or unusually hot battery.

Package safely

Power the phone off, protect it from movement, use a sturdy box, and follow the carrier instructions supplied with the final label or kit.
