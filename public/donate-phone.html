Open the phone donation tool

Before you begin

Gather the phone, identify its model and storage when possible, and decide whether you can access the phone to remove accounts and personal data.

Estimate responsibly

The website’s range is informational, not an appraisal, purchase offer, or guaranteed tax deduction.

Create and print records

The interactive donor tool creates a unique donation ID, packing slip, and pending acknowledgment preview while keeping estimated value off the acknowledgment.

Request mailing materials

Choose a printable prepaid-label request or a mailed-kit request, then review and send the prepared email to Donate by Mail.
