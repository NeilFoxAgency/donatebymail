A supporter campaign without building a device operation from scratch

Phone campaigns for nonprofits

Donate by Mail is developing low-lift campaigns with donor instructions, collection logistics, intake coordination, resale support, and reporting.

Start a phone donation

Campaign materials

Partners receive suggested email, website, social, and supporter messaging that can be adapted to their voice and audience.

Donation logistics

The donor pathway covers device descriptions, data preparation, packing slips, mailing requests, and package identification.

Reporting and proceeds

Campaign reports can distinguish devices received, pathways, gross proceeds, costs, net proceeds, and partner distributions. Participating charities receive 50% of net proceeds.

Explore a pilot

Email Donate by Mail with the organization name, website, supporter audience, campaign goals, and timing. No fixed return is promised before devices are received and inspected.
