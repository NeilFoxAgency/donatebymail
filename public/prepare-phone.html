Before you mail

Prepare your phone safely.

Work through these steps before sealing the package. Keep the phone until anything important has been backed up and you can confirm that account locks are removed.

Start or return to a donation

Five preparation steps

  1. Back up what you want to keep. Save photos, contacts, notes, messages, and authenticator access to iCloud, Google, a computer, or another trusted backup.
  2. Sign out of Apple or Google accounts. This helps remove account ownership from the phone before it changes hands.
  3. Turn off activation locks. Disable Find My iPhone or remove the Android device from the relevant Google account. A locked phone may not be reusable.
  4. Remove SIM and memory cards. Keep physical SIM cards and removable storage. Do not mail passwords, passcodes, or written account credentials.
  5. Factory reset the phone. Reset only after the backup and sign-out steps are complete. If a damaged phone cannot be reset, disclose that in the donation flow.

Find an iPhone model

Open Settings, choose General, then About. Look for Model Name and Capacity. If the phone will not turn on, the model number may appear on the back or SIM tray on some older devices.

Find an Android model

Open Settings and look for About phone or About device. The exact wording varies by manufacturer. Look for Model name, Model number, and Storage.

What not to include

Do not include passcodes, passwords, SIM cards, memory cards, earbuds, cases, unrelated charging accessories, or personal notes containing account information.

Need help?

Review the help and FAQ page or email satoshi@donatebymail.org before mailing the phone.
