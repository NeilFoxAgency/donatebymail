Donation records

Know which document you have.

The packing slip, planning estimate, and charitable acknowledgment serve different purposes. Keeping them separate prevents confusion.

Create a donation packet

Packing slip

Created when you finish the online donation flow. Print it and place it inside the package so the phones can be matched to your donor record. It is not a tax receipt.

Value estimate

An informational range based on the device details you entered. It may help explain potential resale proceeds, but it is not an appraisal and does not appear on the acknowledgment.

Charitable acknowledgment

Issued only after Donate by Mail physically receives and verifies the donated property. It describes the phones and states whether goods or services were provided in exchange.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the acknowledgment not list a dollar value?

For noncash charitable contributions, the donor is responsible for determining and substantiating any fair market value claimed for tax purposes. Donate by Mail acknowledges the property received rather than assigning the donor's tax value.

When will I receive a charitable acknowledgment?

Donate by Mail issues it only after the phones have been physically received and verified. The packing slip and shipping label created before mailing are not charitable acknowledgments.

What should I keep with my records?

Keep the donation ID, packing slip, shipment tracking, final acknowledgment, and any valuation support you relied on. Consult a qualified tax professional for advice about your particular return.

Tax information notice: Donate by Mail provides charitable acknowledgment documents but does not provide individual tax advice or appraisals.
